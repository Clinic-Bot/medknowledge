Welcome, Academic Institutions

A learning initiative built for your students, not just another app

MedKnowledge is an educational initiative developed to make medical education more accessible through artificial intelligence. It brings structured notes, daily practice questions and clinical case discussions into one place — free of cost, for every student who needs it.

We built it because students preparing for Medical, Nursing and Allied Health careers deserve consistent, high-quality study support outside the classroom. Colleges and faculty are invited to share MedKnowledge with their students as a supplementary learning resource alongside their existing curriculum.

  • An AI study assistant available whenever a student needs an explanation, at 2pm or 2am.
  • Structured notes across Anatomy, Physiology, Pharmacology, Pathology and more.
  • Daily MCQs that build exam readiness for NEET, university and licensing exams.
  • Shared free with your entire student body — no licensing fee for the institution.
